My story

Local roots. Real numbers.

I grew up in Barrie and graduated from St. Joseph's High School in 2006. My first business was Barrie Iron, a gym I owned and ran from 2013 to 2017. Running it taught me what every small business owner learns fast: show up, keep your word, and know your numbers.

In 2017 I became a mortgage agent, and I've spent nearly a decade since then arranging purchases, renewals, refinances and private lending for families across the region. I've seen what happens when the financing is an afterthought: deals that fall apart, buyers who overpay, sellers stuck between two homes.

That's why I got my real estate licence in 2026. Now the home and the mortgage are planned together from day one, by the same person, with your whole financial picture in view.

Off the clock

Family first. Community always.

Home is Horseshoe Highlands, with my wife, our two boys and our dog, Ozzy. We make the most of everything Horseshoe Valley has to offer: snowboarding all winter, hiking the trails all summer, and as much time outside together as we can get. When the house finally goes quiet, you'll usually find me with a guitar in my hands.

Since January 2025, I've also been a volunteer firefighter with Oro-Medonte Fire & Emergency Services at Station 3 in Horseshoe Valley. Firefighting and real estate have more in common than you'd think: stay calm under pressure, prepare for what can go wrong, and show up when it matters.
