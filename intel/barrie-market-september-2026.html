Barrie right now: buyer's or seller's market?

Short answer: buyers have the edge. Homes are taking over a month to sell, most go for less than asking, and there's a lot of supply around the county.

How to read it

Three numbers tell you who has the upper hand.

1. Prices are lower than a year ago

The benchmark price, which tracks a "typical" Barrie home, is down 5.1% year over year. That's the cleanest read on value because it isn't skewed by which homes happened to sell that month.

2. Homes sell under asking

At 98% of list price, the average Barrie seller took about 2% less than asking. On a $650,000 listing, that's roughly $13,000 of negotiating room for a buyer. In a seller's market, that number sits at or above 100%.

3. Homes take more than a month to sell

40 days on average. Buyers have time to compare, come back for a second showing and make conditional offers, things that disappear in a hot market.

With 4,305 homes for sale and 611 sold in the month, the county has roughly 7 months of inventory. As a rule of thumb, under 4 months favours sellers, 4 to 6 is balanced, and over 6 favours buyers.

One thing to watch: new listings dropped almost 14% from last year. Fewer sellers are listing, which slowly eats into that supply. If that keeps up into spring, conditions can tighten fast.

Verdict: it's a buyer's market across Simcoe County. Barrie is a little firmer than the county as a whole, with homes selling faster (40 days vs. 46) and close to asking, but buyers still have room to negotiate.

My take

Buyers: you have real negotiating room right now. Get pre-approved, take your time, and don't be shy about conditions on financing and inspection.

Sellers: price it right from day one. Overpriced homes are the ones sitting 60, 90, 100+ days and selling for less in the end. Preparation and pricing matter more than ever.
