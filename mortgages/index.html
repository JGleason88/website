Is this right for you?

It works brilliantly, for the right person.

  • You need to spend less than you earn. Your spare cash is what pays the mortgage down. It's a line of credit, so overspending makes the balance grow instead.
  • The rate is usually variable. If rates rise, your interest rises with them.
  • You need to qualify, including the stress test, and have enough equity in your home.
  • It rewards discipline. Your full borrowing limit is always available, which is great for emergencies and risky if spending creeps up.

For illustration only. The current plan uses your payment with semi-annual compounding, and your other debts at their own rates and payments. "Prepaying" puts your monthly spare cash, freed-up debt payments and lump sums against your current mortgage, limited to an assumed 20% of the starting balance per year. The all-in-one deposits your pay on each payday, spends your expenses evenly through the month, charges interest daily on the balance at the rate shown and posts it monthly, and adds the monthly fee; your rate is assumed not to change. Excludes penalties to break your current mortgage, legal and appraisal costs, and any changes to your income or spending. Not a commitment to lend; requires approval.
