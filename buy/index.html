Get approved for more

Your credit score

Canadian scores run from 300 to 900. A stronger score means more lenders, better rates and an easier approval.

  • Pay every bill on time; set up autopay for minimums
  • Keep card balances under 30% of the limit (under 10% is even better)
  • Keep your oldest cards open and avoid new credit applications before you buy
  • Check your reports for free with Equifax and TransUnion, and dispute errors

Buyer questions

Good questions.

How much do I need for a down payment?

At least 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the portion above it, up to $1.5 million. Under 20% down, you'll pay mortgage default insurance, which is added to the mortgage. Plan for closing costs on top, usually around 1.5% of the price.

What's the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?

A pre-qualification is a quick estimate. A pre-approval means a lender has reviewed your income, credit and down payment and is holding a rate for you, usually 90 to 120 days. It's what lets you make a confident offer.

Does a pre-approval hurt my credit score?

A lender credit check is a "hard" inquiry and can lower your score by a few points for a short time. That's why I do one check and shop it to many lenders, instead of you applying at several banks.

Do I pay you as a buyer?

In most purchases, the buyer's agent is paid out of the sale, not by you directly. We'll go through exactly how it works for your purchase in our buyer representation agreement before we start, so there are no surprises.
